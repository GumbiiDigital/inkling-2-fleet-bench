The 1M-Context Breakthrough (1,047,040 Tokens)

Why every engine appeared to fail at 1M on standard chat—and the exact chat-template workaround that unlocked 3/3 recall.

Root Cause & Fix: Inkling-2-Mini's chat template ignores reasoning_effort=none (~98.4% reasoning tokens). At 1M context, every engine burns its 2K/8K output budget looping in reasoning. Passing assistant prefill (continue_final_message) bypasses the reasoning loop and yields 3/3 exact hits (XJ9-4782, XJ9-6498, XJ9-7378) in 9 output tokens on DGX Spark vLLM max-bf16.
